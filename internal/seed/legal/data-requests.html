How to see, correct, download or delete the personal data Codevertex Africa Limited holds about you.

1. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 (and the GDPR where it applies to you) you can:

  • See your data. Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct it. Fix anything that is wrong or out of date. Most details can be edited on your Profile.
  • Get a copy. Receive your data in a common, machine-readable format to keep or move elsewhere.
  • Delete it. Ask us to delete your account and personal data, except records the law says we must keep.
  • Stop marketing. Opt out of marketing at any time, free of charge, and withdraw any consent you gave.
  • Object or complain. Object to a use of your data, and complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

2. How to make a request

Email legal@codevertexafrica.com from the address on your account, and say which right you are using (for example "send me a copy of my data" or "delete my account"). To protect you, we may ask you to confirm your identity before acting.

We do not charge for requests and reply as soon as we can, within the time the law sets.

3. What deletion means

Deleting your account removes your sign-in and personal details from every Codevertex app. Some records have to be kept for a set time by law, for example tax invoices and accounting records, and weighbridge enforcement records. Those are kept only as long as required.

If you run a business on Codevertex, the records of your own customers belong to your business. Ask the business directly about their data, or contact us if you cannot reach them.

4. Data from Facebook, Instagram and Threads

If you connected a Facebook Page, Instagram account, ad account or Threads profile to MarketFlow, you can delete what we received from Meta for you in two ways:

  • Remove MarketFlow from your Facebook settings (Settings and privacy, Settings, Apps and websites) or from Threads. Meta tells us at once, we delete the access tokens and connections you granted, and Meta shows you a confirmation code.
  • Or email legal@codevertexafrica.com and we do it for you.

You can check a request with its confirmation code at marketflow.codevertexafrica.com/data-deletion. Leads and messages that people sent to a business stay with that business as its customer records.

5. Data from YouTube and TikTok

If you connected a YouTube channel or a TikTok account to MarketFlow, you can delete what we received for it in any of these ways:

  • Disconnect the channel or account in MarketFlow, under Settings, Integrations.
  • For YouTube, revoke MarketFlow's access on the Google security settings page. For TikTok, remove MarketFlow from the apps that have access to your TikTok account in the TikTok app's settings.
  • Or email legal@codevertexafrica.com and we do it for you.

We delete the access tokens at once, and the channel or account details and the records of what MarketFlow posted within 7 days. Videos already published stay on YouTube or TikTok until you delete them there. What we store is listed in Sections 5D and 5E of our Privacy Policy.

6. Stopping marketing messages

You can stop marketing at any time, free of charge: use the unsubscribe link or reply STOP where a message offers it, or email legal@codevertexafrica.com. Service messages you need, such as invoices, receipts and security codes, are not marketing and continue.

7. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handled your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya at odpc.go.ke. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why.
