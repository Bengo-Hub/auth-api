Part 1. Summary

  1. When the veto window ends, the agent posts the finished video to the accounts you chose, unless you or an admin cancel it first.
  2. Before each post we email you what will go out, where and when, with a link that cancels it. Anyone holding that link can cancel.
  3. Paid boosts and ads never start by themselves. Each one needs your approval and a funded ad account.
  4. A video in a restricted category, in one of your hold categories, flagged by the compliance review or over your credit ceiling waits for your approval.
  5. The videos are made with AI and go out under your business name. A post you did not cancel counts as approved by you.
  6. Switching the agent off, or back to approving each ad, cancels every post still waiting.
  7. When these terms change you accept them again. Until then every video waits for your approval.

3. What Auto-Publish does

3.1 Auto-Publish is off until you turn it on. It is available only if your plan includes it and the current version of these terms has been accepted.

3.2 When it is on, for each run the Agent:

(a) writes a video script from the offerings, objectives and settings you chose;

(b) for presenter videos, makes a storyboard and approves that storyboard itself;

(c) renders the video, charging Credits within your Credit Ceiling;

(d) schedules an Organic Post on the Connected Accounts you selected, to go out when the Veto Window ends; and

(e) sends the Heads-up Email.

3.3 If nobody cancels the post during the Veto Window, it is published. Our records show it as approved because the Veto Window passed, and do not name a person as having approved it.

3.4 The Agent only publishes Organic Posts. It never starts, funds or changes a Paid Promotion (see clause 6.1).

3.5 Before each run we check again that your plan includes the feature and that the current terms are accepted. If either has lapsed, that run waits for a person to approve each video.

5. The Veto Window and how to cancel

5.1 Each Organic Post waits for the full Veto Window before it is published.

5.2 During the Veto Window, a post can be cancelled:

(a) by an Approver, from the Agent's scheduled posts in the app; or

(b) by anyone holding the Cancel Link, through the confirmation page it opens.

Team members who are not Approvers can see the countdown but cannot cancel.

5.3 One cancel, in the app or by Cancel Link, stops the post on every Connected Account it was scheduled for. In the Studio you can also cancel it on one account at a time; the post then still goes out on the accounts you did not cancel.

5.4 Once a post has reached a platform it can no longer be cancelled in MarketFlow, and the app or the confirmation page will say it is too late. You can still remove it:

(a) Delete from platform. Where the platform lets us delete a post we published, the account owner and admins can delete it from the posting calendar in the Studio, after confirming. Today that covers Facebook Pages and YouTube, and Instagram once the connected account has given us permission to delete posts. The post is removed for everyone, with its views, likes and comments, and it cannot be restored. We record who deleted it and when.

(b) Delete it on the platform. TikTok does not let apps delete a post, and neither does a platform whose connected account has not given us that permission. For those posts the Studio shows the platform's own steps, and you delete the post there.

(c) If a platform refuses a delete, we show you its reason and the post stays where it is until you try again or delete it on the platform. Platforms change what they allow, so the list in (a) may change; we keep the Studio up to date with it.

5.5 If we cannot send the Heads-up Email for a post, that post is not Auto-Published and waits for your approval instead.

5.6 We aim to publish as close to the end of the Veto Window as we can, but the exact time depends on the platform and on our systems. A post may go out a little later than scheduled. It will never go out before the Veto Window ends.
